Chinese pop-rock in the style of Wang Heye's "热铁皮房顶的夏天" band sound: a nostalgic, sincere, band-driven coming-of-age song about leaving campus, chasing a career in the big city, and growing up, around 104 BPM, major key with a wistful bittersweet undertone. Male lead vocal: warm, slightly raspy and honest, youthful and gritty, conversational storytelling in the verses, then an anthemic sing-along chorus with layered harmonies and crowd-style gang vocals in the final chorus. Arrangement: clean electric guitar arpeggios and ringing chords, acoustic strumming, overdriven electric guitar in the chorus, warm round bass, live rock drums with a solid backbeat, piano and organ pad, soaring strings in the climax. Structure: short solo guitar intro, intimate verse, rising pre-chorus, big anthemic chorus, fuller second verse, bridge stripped back to guitar and voice, final chorus biggest with gang vocals, outro returning to a single guitar. Mood: nostalgic, hopeful, bittersweet, the warmth of a summer night, the loneliness and ambition of a young adult chasing a career, moved but not defeated. Production: warm analog tape character, wide natural reverb, punchy but clean drums, vocal forward. No EDM, no synth-pop, no rap, no heavy metal.
