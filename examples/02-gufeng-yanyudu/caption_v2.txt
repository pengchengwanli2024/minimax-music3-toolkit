Chinese-style (zhongguo feng) cinematic pop ballad in the spirit of Jay Chou's "Fireworks Cool Easily": melancholy minor key, around 72 BPM, modern R&B-tinged vocal phrasing and relaxed rhythmic delivery, ancient Jiangnan storytelling mood, dramatic and epic but intimate. GRAND CINEMATIC INTRO (about 14 seconds): distant rain ambience, a lone dizi motif, deep taiko-style drum hits, a sweeping guzheng glissando, strings rising in a slow crescendo, building real momentum before the vocal enters. Male lead vocal: warm, slightly nasal tenor with clear expressive Chinese enunciation, conversational phrasing in the verses, restrained at first, then a PASSIONATE, soaring, high-energy chorus with layered harmonies, octave doubling and tasteful ad-libs; the chorus must be simple, repetitive, hooky and instantly memorable, easy to sing along after one listen. Instruments: dizi, xiao, guzheng, pipa, erhu, grand piano, cinematic strings, taiko drums, plus a subtle modern sub bass and soft programmed R&B drums to modernize the groove. Structure: grand intro, intimate verse, building pre-chorus, huge catchy chorus, second verse with pipa, pre-chorus, chorus, a stripped bridge with piano and xiao, a final bigger chorus with full harmonies and ad-libs, then a quiet outro of dizi and rain. Mood: epic, sorrowful, nostalgic, unfulfilled love across ten years. Mix: wide cinematic reverb, punchy but clean low end, vocal forward. No rap, no screaming, no EDM drops, no heavy rock guitars.
