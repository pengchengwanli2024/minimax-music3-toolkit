Chinese ancient-style (gufeng) cinematic ballad, around 68 BPM, sorrowful and nostalgic, set in Jiangnan in the misty rain. Male lead vocal: warm, slightly weathered tenor with restrained emotion, breathy intimate verses and an open, aching chorus, very subtle Chinese opera-tinged ornamentation used sparingly, natural emotional cracks. Traditional instruments: solo dizi bamboo flute, guzheng, pipa, erhu, xiao, soft large drum, deep strings and a gentle atmospheric pad, with rain ambience under the intro and outro. Structure: sparse intro with dizi and rain, intimate verse with guzheng, pre-chorus joined by erhu, chorus opening with strings and drum, second verse with pipa plucks, bridge stripped back to xiao and voice, final chorus full and aching, outro returning to dizi and rain. Mood: tender, bittersweet, unfulfilled love, longing across ten years, misty Jiangnan, holding back tears. Polished cinematic production with warm low end and a wide natural reverb. No EDM, no rap, no modern pop drums, no over-singing, no heavy percussion.
